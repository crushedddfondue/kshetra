# Kshetra

> Spatial Intelligence for the physical world

Turning observations of the physical world into a persistent, queryable understanding of what exists, what changed, and what matters.

---

## The Problem

The physical world changes continuously, but the digital representation of it is often fragmented.

In construction and interior design, project information is distributed across:

- Photographs
- Videos
- Drawings
- CAD/BIM files
- PDFs
- Spreadsheets
- Messaging applications
- Manual reports
- Site visits

There may be knowledge about how a sight looked on a particular day, but there is no persistent computational representation of **what the sire looks like ?**

This creates several problems:

- Limited visibility between site visits
- Manual progress documentation
- Difficulty comparing reality against previous states
- Delayed discovery of deviations
- Fragmented project knowledge
- Expensive coordination and reporting

The fundamental problem is therefore:

> **How do we give software a persistent understanding of a physical environment?**

---

## The Idea

Kshetra treats the physical environment as a **state that evolves over time**.
